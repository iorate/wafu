The default keys no longer use function keys, and modifiers now follow one pattern. `palette` moves from `F1` to `?`, `reload` from `F5` to `,`, `rename_template` from `Alt+R` to `Ctrl+Shift+R`, `enter_as_archive` from `Shift+U` to `Shift+V`, and `new_file` from `N` to `Shift+K`; `swap_panes` (`Alt+O`), `clear_log` (`Alt+D`), and `copy_log` (`Alt+Y`) get default keys.
