Archives in tar, tar.gz (tgz), tar.bz2 (tbz2), and tar.xz (txz) can be browsed, previewed, extracted, and made with compress, alongside zip. The format of a new archive follows the extension typed in the compress popup; only zip takes a password, and a password typed for a tar archive is refused with an error. enter_as_archive and extract detect the format from the file's contents when the extension does not identify it.
