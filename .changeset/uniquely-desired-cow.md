Archives in tar, tar.gz (tgz), tar.bz2 (tbz2), and tar.xz (txz) can be browsed, previewed, extracted, and made with compress, next to zip. The format of a new archive follows the extension typed in the compress popup; only zip takes a password. enter_as_archive and extract read the format off the content when the extension does not tell.
